# Code of Conduct

Keep RAM Clean is a place to build and learn together. Everyone is welcome,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## How we work together

- Critique the work, not the person. Explain the problem, cite evidence, and
  leave room for different experience levels and setups.
- Assume good faith without ignoring impact. Listen when someone raises a
  concern; give people room to correct a mistake.
- Respect consent and privacy. Don't repost private conversations or personal
  information without permission.
- Harassment, discrimination, sexual harassment, threats, intimidation,
  targeted insults, and retaliation for reporting a concern are not acceptable.

These standards apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
spaces used for official project work. They also apply when someone represents
the project in public.

## Report a concern

Contact the repository maintainer privately using the
[maintainer contact already listed in Cargo.toml](Cargo.toml). Please do not
file a public issue about a person or incident. Include what happened, where
and when, and any relevant context you are comfortable sharing. If the concern
involves the maintainer or you do not feel safe contacting them, you can use
[GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for behavior on GitHub.

The maintainer will limit sharing to what is needed to handle the report.
Depending on the situation, the maintainer may ask for a correction, remove
content, restrict participation, or block an account from the repository.
Serious or repeated behavior may warrant immediate action.
